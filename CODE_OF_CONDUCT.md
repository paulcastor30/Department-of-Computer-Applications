# Code of Conduct

Treat people respectfully in issues, pull requests, and reviews. Beginners should be able to ask questions without being mocked.

- Give feedback about the work, with examples that help the author improve it.
- Respect differences in experience and opinion.
- Credit other people's work and respect their privacy.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or personally attack others.
- Do not share someone's private information without permission or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

The repository owner, [@paulcastor30](https://github.com/paulcastor30), moderates repository discussions and can remove abusive content or restrict participation.

A private reporting contact has not been published yet. It needs to be added here; do not put sensitive reports in public issues.
